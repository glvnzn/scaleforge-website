### Requirements

* At least 3 years of hands-on experience with ReactJS
* Proficiency in NodeJS and Typescript
* Knowledge of state management tools (zustand, redux, context, etc.)
* Experience in Unit and Integration Testing using Jest, React Testing Library, and other testing libraries
* Understanding of RESTful APIs/GraphQL and integration of endpoints to frontend
* Familiarity with frontend frameworks such as Next.js, CRA
* Understanding of design patterns, software designing, and architecture best practices
* Able to think strategically and develop scalable solutions.
* Good attention to UI/interaction/animation details.
* Experience collaborating with designers within a customer-centric design process and a passion for building amazing user-facing products.
* Familiar with GIT flow and other SCM systems

### Nice to Have

* ChakraUI, TailwindCSS, and UI/UX experiences
* Experience in converting Figma designs to rich-quality code
* Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
* Excellent communication and teamwork abilities
* Strong understanding of different rendering methods (SSR, SSG, CSR, ISR)
* Familiarity with component modularization
* Experience in integration of third-party APIs and Services
* Experience in tackling web performance optimizations measures (Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals)
* Knowledge of NPM package creation
* Knowledge of Storybook
* Experience with agile software development methodologies
* Have a good knowledge of UI, responsive design, and cross-browser compatibility.
* Knowledge of other frontend frameworks and paradigms (Svelte and SvelteKit, React Native/Flutter)

### Key Responsibilities

* Lead projects and initiatives with different and competing timelines and requirements.
* Collaborate with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to design and implement user-facing features
* Design and develop technical solutions to support ScaleForge's business operations.
* Create reusable components and place them into a curated repository Storybook/Git/NPM for other developers to create their digital applications
* Work within the existing framework but always suggest improvements in how we work and choice of platforms/technologies.
* Optimizing components for maximum performance across a vast array of web-capable devices and browsers
* Coordinating with various teams working on distinct layers
* Stay informed and in tune with the latest methodologies, best practices, and trends in the web/ app development
* Optimize application performance and ensure seamless user experience across various devices and browsers
* Participate in code reviews to maintain code quality and adhere to best practices.
* Identify and address technical debt, and proactively suggest improvements to existing systems.
* Mentor and provide technical guidance to other front-end engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and growth.
* Write clean, reusable, and well-documented code
* Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications
* Work with limited direction in a fluid environment while exceeding delivery expectations and service levels.
* Contribute to ongoing maintenance of existing and future projects.
* Foster a transparent and collaborative environment within the development team and throughout the entire organization.
